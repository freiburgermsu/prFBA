For the original panel, the only d20 exact-tie scoring is the June 11 variant set (`region_validation/data/taxacc_region_summary_et.json`, `gene_capture_by_region_et.json`). It predates the September/October scorer corrections: it has no `select_mode`, no `correct_population` and no unscoreable-rank exclusion. It is therefore not like-for-like, and I use it only as a qualitative check in M10 |

For the mixed three-panel citations I also built a **bridge**: the identical mixed pairing
at d100 (original L100, k-means and random U100). It was computed with
`three_panel/compare_three_panels.py --roots ... --out <scratchpad>` and written only to my
scratchpad. The bridge is **more** distorting at d100 than the pairing was at d20, because
the union rule's include-mode quality drops at depth 100 while legacy's does not (see §3,
item M10). I quote it only to show that effect. **The revision should cite single-rule
files**, U100 or L100, not a mixed pairing.

The hits records, and therefore SELF_TOP20, SELF_SAT, SELF_CRED, the emptied-record shares
and the tie statistics, are rule-independent. The legacy trees hold byte-identical copies of
the union trees' hits; check "copy … identical=True" confirms this.
